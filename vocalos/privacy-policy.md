# Vocal OS — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

Vocal OS is a vocal training and music education application. This policy explains how the app handles microphone audio, recordings, imported MIDI files, training progress, and settings.

## Microphone

Vocal OS requests microphone access only for user-initiated vocal features such as Voice Check, Trainer, Games, Mic Doctor, and manual recording. Microphone access is used to estimate pitch, stability, dynamics, timing, and other training feedback in real time.

Audio analysis is performed locally on the user's Android device. Vocal OS does not upload microphone audio to a server and does not share microphone audio with third parties.

## Recordings

A recording is created only when the user explicitly starts the recording feature. Recordings are stored in the application's private local storage. They are not uploaded or shared by Vocal OS.

## Training data and settings

The app stores profile settings, Voice Check results, training progress, history, accuracy preferences, course progress, achievements, and related application state locally on the device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

## MIDI and files

When the user chooses a MIDI file through the Android system file picker, Vocal OS reads that selected file for local song/practice analysis. The selected file is not uploaded by the app.

## Network, advertising and analytics

The current Vocal OS release does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cloud accounts, or remote tracking. The app therefore does not transmit user data over the network.

## Data sharing and sale

Vocal OS does not sell user data. The current release does not share personal or sensitive user data with third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the user's device until the user clears Vocal OS application data or uninstalls the app. Recordings stored in the app's private storage are removed when application data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

## Voice and health information

Vocal OS is an educational vocal-training tool and is not a medical device. Training feedback is not a diagnosis. Users should stop an exercise if they experience pain, burning, sudden or persistent hoarseness, loss of voice, or other concerning symptoms and seek an appropriate qualified professional when needed.

## Children

Vocal OS is not designed as a child-directed service and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The Google Play target-audience declaration should reflect the audience selected by the publisher.

## Changes

If the app later adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, online backups, or any other network-based data processing, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before those features are released.
